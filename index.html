About The Artisan's Table:

At the Artisan's table, we believe good food brings people together. Based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, we prepare delicious Nigerian meals using fresh ingredients, traditional recipes, and a passion for great food. From our favorite local dishes to beautifully presented meals, every plate is prepared with care, bringing familiar flavors to your table.

Our passion goes beyond serving food. We create welcoming experiences where friends and families can share memorable meals, and beginners can discover the joy of Nigerian cooking through our cooking classes. Whether you're ordering a meal or learning a new recipe, you are always welcome at The Artisans Table, where Nigerian culinary heritage meets genuine hospitality.

Learn The Art Of Nigerian Cooking

At The Artisan's Table we believe that cooking is more than preparing a meal; it is an opportunity to express creativity, preserve traditions, and bring people together. Our cooking classes are designed for anyone who wants to explore the richness of Nigerian cuisine, Whether you are a complete beginner or someone looking to improve your skills in the kitchen.

Learn how to prepare delicious Nigerian favorites while discovering the techniques, ingredients, and little details that make each dish special. From understanding the right combination of spices to achieving the perfect texture and balancing flavors, we want you to develop the knowledge and confidence to recreate wonderful meals in your own kitchen. Our approach focuses on practical learning, giving you the opportunity to understand not just how a dish is prepared, but why each step matters.

Beyond the recipes, our classes offer an opportunity to appreciate the culinary heritage behind the food we Enjoy. You discover the key creativity and traditions that make Nigerian cooking so distinctive, all within a welcoming environment where questions are encouraged and learning is part of the experience. Whether you're hoping to become more independent in the kitchen, surprise your family with a delicious meal, or simply explore a new passion, there's something rewarding about learning to create good food with your own hands
